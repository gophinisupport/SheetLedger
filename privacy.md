# SheetLedger – Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 18, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how SheetLedger ("we", "our", or "us") handles your information. SheetLedger is built as a privacy-first, decentralized, and serverless utility application. Our core philosophy is simple: **Your financial data belongs to you, and we have absolutely no access to it.**

By using SheetLedger, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

---

### 1. No Collection of Financial or Personal Data
SheetLedger is a local-first application. Unlike traditional bookkeeping apps, we **do not** own, operate, or maintain any central servers. 
- **Your Financial Records:** All transaction logs, account balances, and bookkeeping entries are stored locally on your device in a SQLite database.
- **No Server Interception:** We do not collect, read, transmit, or monetize your personal financial information. Your bookkeeping data never touches any third-party infrastructure controlled by us and is never shared with advertising networks.

### 2. Google Drive & Google Sheets Integration
To provide cloud synchronization and backup, SheetLedger allows you to connect your personal Google Account. 
- **Data Scopes:** The app requests restricted access to your Google Drive and Google Sheets solely to create, sync, and manage the dedicated encrypted spreadsheets generated by SheetLedger.
- **End-to-End Client-Side Encryption:** All bookkeeping records are encrypted locally on your device before being synced to Google Sheets. Your Google Sheets only store encrypted ciphertext, ensuring your financial data remains unreadable even within your cloud storage without your local decryption key.
- **Direct Communication:** All data transmission occurs directly between your device and Google’s official APIs. We do not act as a middleman, and we have no ability to view your Google account credentials, encryption keys, or synced files.
- **Data Ownership:** You maintain full ownership of the spreadsheet files stored in your Google Drive. You can back up, export, or delete these files directly from your Google Drive at any time.

### 3. In-App Purchases and Billing (RevenueCat)
We use RevenueCat to manage premium subscriptions and in-app purchases.
- When you purchase a subscription or upgrade via Google Play, anonymized transaction data (such as product identifiers, purchase time, and subscription status) is processed securely to unlock your premium features and remove advertisements.
- No personal identity or specific financial payment methods (such as credit card numbers) are ever shared with or stored by us.

### 4. Advertising in the Free Version (Google AdMob)
To support the ongoing development and maintenance of SheetLedger, the free version of the app displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**.
- **Data Collected by Google:** Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain device and usage data—such as your device's Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, general device information, and ad interaction metrics—to serve and measure advertisements.
- **Complete Isolation from Your Finances:** AdMob operates independently from your local database and Google Sheets. Your financial entries, categories, and balances are **never** accessed by or shared with Google AdMob.
- **Managing Ad Preferences & Ad-Free Option:** You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads**). Additionally, upgrading to the premium version of SheetLedger completely disables all advertisements and stops any ad-related network requests.

### 5. Device Permissions
The app may request the following permissions only to ensure core functionality:
- **Internet Access:** Required to synchronize your encrypted local database with your personal Google Sheets, verify Google Play billing status, and load advertisements in the free version of the app.

### 6. Third-Party Services
While SheetLedger itself does not collect or store your personal data, it utilizes trusted third-party services that may collect information under their respective privacy policies:
- [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google AdMob & Advertising Privacy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

### 7. Compliance with GDPR (European Users)
Because SheetLedger does not store, process, or control any personal data on any servers, we adhere strictly to the principles of data minimization and privacy-by-design under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). 
- **Your Financial Data:** Remains encrypted and stored solely on your device or within your personal Google Drive, giving you absolute control over data deletion and portability.
- **Advertising Consent:** For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) will ask for your consent regarding personalized or non-personalized ads upon first launch, and you can revoke or modify your consent preferences at any time.

### 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect technical, feature, or regulatory changes. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
